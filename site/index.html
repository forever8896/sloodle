Hey Stupid, it's time to learn.

sloodle is the harness for learning anything. Sloo, a plush owl tutor, researches any subject you name, plans a course around you and teaches it in an interactive chat with diagrams, runnable code, flashcards and more. It speaks aloud with a local voice and remembers how you learn.

Courses share as one open JSON file. Everything runs and is stored on your computer, it is open source under the MIT licence, and it runs on the Claude subscription you already have.
